## 2. QA and common failures

Method: QA never judges the native file. Export every surface to PNG at 1x (PDF pages via the print dialogue or `pdftoppm -r 96`), overlay the safe area from the table as a 1 px guide layer, and run the passes in `../../qa/PLAYBOOK.md` section 4 that apply: measurement (type size in px or pt against the minimum, word count, mark size and clear space), alignment (edges on the grid columns), copy (tone words present, forbidden word absent, sentence case) and accessibility (every text pair through `scripts/contrast.py` at 4.5:1, large text at 3:1). Then view at real scale: a deck slide at 25 percent zoom on a 27-inch screen approximates 6 m; a card at 100 percent on a retina display approximates arm's length; a poster at 10 percent approximates 2 m. The mark is squint-tested at its minimum size on every surface. `[house]`

| Social square and portrait | Text 54 px or larger; content inside the safe rectangle; the wordmark 24 px or taller; 1 of the 3 layouts, unaltered; contrast on the photograph region measured | 1 Text in the 1:1 grid crop zone (y under 135 or over 1215 on portrait). 2 Text at 36 px "to fit". 3 A fourth layout invented for one post |

## 3. Deck templates

Frame 1920 x 1080. Column starts: 96, 242, 388, 534, 680, 826, 972, 1118, 1264, 1410, 1556, 1702; the grid ends at x 1824. Vertical zones: title y 96 to 160; content y 224 to 984; footer y 1000 to 1024. All text is 24 pt or larger; every element's left edge is a column start; the theme is light or dark for the whole deck. Sizes are in pt where 1 pt equals 1 px in the frame. `[source: Duarte, 2008 for one idea per slide; house positions]`

## 5. Conversions

- Points to pixels in a 1920 x 1080 frame: 1 pt = 1 px (Keynote and PowerPoint at 1920 x 1080 pt). At 1280 x 720, multiply sizes by 0.667.
- Millimetres to pixels at 300 dpi: multiply mm by 11.81 (85 x 55 mm = 1004 x 650 px; A4 = 2480 x 3508 px; A2 = 4961 x 7016 px). `[source: ISO 216; 300 dpi]`
- dp to px for Android adaptive icons: 108 dp = 432 px at xxxhdpi (4x); the 66 dp safe circle = 264 px. `[source: Android adaptive icons]`
- Viewing distance to cap height for signage: 25 mm per 3 m (about 1 inch per 10 feet); double it for text that must be read while moving. `[house convention]`
- Screen accent to print: convert the OKLCH accent to hex, then to CMYK with the printer's profile (FOGRA39 for coated stock in Europe, GRACoL for the US), print a proof, and record the build and the nearest Pantone beside the hex in `brand/colour/print.md`. Never trust the application's automatic conversion for a saturated accent. `[house]`
