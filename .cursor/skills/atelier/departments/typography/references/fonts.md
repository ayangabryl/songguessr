# Typeface catalogue

Loaded on demand from `../PLAYBOOK.md` section 3.1. Two tables, then notes on reading a specimen, eight pairing recipes and the slop signals. Licences and URLs were checked against the source linked in each row (2026); re-check before shipping, because Fontshare and Pangram Pangram terms change more often than OFL fonts do.

Licence key: OFL 1.1 is open source (use, modify, redistribute; not sold alone; reserved font names apply to modified versions) `[source: SIL OFL 1.1]`. Fontshare "ITF FFL" is free for personal and commercial use but closed source: no modification, no redistribution, no passing files to agencies; self-hosting on your own servers is permitted `[source: Fontshare licence page]`. Apache 2.0 and MIT fonts are open source; none of the required families below use them, but Roboto (Apache 2.0) appears in the slop list.

Overuse risk: low, medium, high. High means the family reads as template output in 2026 regardless of how well it is set. `[house]`

Class key: grotesk, humanist, geometric, neo-grotesk, transitional serif, old-style, didone, slab, mono, rounded.

## C. Notes

### C.1 Reading a specimen in five minutes

1. Test string at 16 px and 13 px: `Illegal 1Il| 0O rn m — 0123456789 £€$ fi fl ?! "quotes" Aa Gg Rr`. Reject if `I`, `l` and `1` or `0` and `O` are not distinguishable at 13 px. `[house]`
2. Numerals: check for `tnum` (tabular) and `lnum` (lining). Set `0123456789` twice with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` and compare widths; if the digits still shift, the feature is missing and the face fails as a body face. `[house]`
3. Italic: confirm a true italic exists (different `a`, `f`, `e` skeleton), not an oblique. With `font-synthesis: none` a missing italic renders upright; that is the honest result. `[house]`
4. Weights: confirm a real 500 or 600 between 400 and 700. A family with only 400 and 700 forces bold labels. `[house]`
5. Hinting at 13 px on Windows: render the test string in Chrome and Edge on Windows (or the QA capture matrix) at 13 px and 100 percent zoom; stems must be even and no glyph may drop a pixel row. Unhinted variable fonts often fail here. `[house]`
6. Optical size: if the family has `opsz`, set 13 px and 49 px side by side; spacing and contrast should differ visibly. If they do not, the axis is nominal. `[source: MDN variable fonts]`
7. File size and axes for engineering: note woff2 size per style and which axes exist; `../../engineering/PLAYBOOK.md` decides files and fallbacks. `[house]`

### C.3 Slop signals

Families whose presence, as defaults, makes a design read as template output in 2026. Each is acceptable only with a written reason in `concept.md` and a direction that depends on it. `[house]`

- Space Grotesk, Poppins, Montserrat, Playfair Display: the four the front desk names; each has been the default of a generator or theme.
- Roboto (Apache 2.0) and Open Sans as brand faces: system and Android defaults, invisible as identity.
- Inter as the only family on a brand or portfolio site; Inter + Roboto together.
- Plus Jakarta Sans, Outfit, Urbanist, Satoshi, General Sans, Clash Display: the 2021 to 2026 startup landing-page set.
- DM Sans + DM Serif Display as a pair: the theme-store pairing.
- Fraunces or Instrument Serif as a single hero line above a grotesk body with no second appearance: costume, not system.
- Lato, Raleway, Nunito, Quicksand, Oswald, Bebas Neue, Lobster, Pacifico: the 2012 to 2018 Google Fonts set; Bebas Neue and Oswald tracked caps in particular.
- Any mono headline on a consumer product; any pixel font outside a deliberate retro direction.
- Gradient-filled display text in any family.
